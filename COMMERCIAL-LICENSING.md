NaOnDB is free for the noncommercial purposes in [LICENSE](LICENSE). Commercial
use requires a separate written agreement and payment of the agreed license fee.
This page states the current licensing policy; it is **not** a complete commercial
contract, an automatic payment-to-license mechanism, or a penalty schedule.

## What needs a commercial license?

Examples include a commercial application incorporating NaOnDB, SaaS or a hosted
graph service, paid customer projects, product development for anticipated
commercial deployment, and internal use supporting commercial business operations.
A free tier, a pre-revenue startup, or a nonprofit legal form is not an exemption.
The activity-based definitions and research/contribution exceptions in LICENSE control.

Personal noncommercial projects, noncommercial teaching and research, public
research results, and qualifying contributions to NaOnDB remain free. You do not
need to buy a license merely to submit a patch. The community license does not
require you to publish private modifications.

## Obtaining permission

Contact **Christian Klose / @ChrisDelClea** through the
[NaOnDB repository](https://github.com/ChrisDelClea/NaOnDB).
A public issue titled `Commercial license inquiry` may be used to request a private
contact channel. Do not post payment details, billing addresses, confidential
business information, or signed agreements in public issues.

Before commercial use, the parties must agree in writing on the license scope
and payment terms. The current license fee is **EUR 20,000**. The written quote
must make any applicable VAT treatment and the total payable explicit before
acceptance; this page does not determine tax treatment or issue an invoice.
Payment details are provided directly by Christian Klose under that agreement.

## Scope must be agreed, not assumed

A commercial agreement must identify the licensee and any covered affiliates,
the product or internal use, covered versions, deployment and distribution rights,
territory, term, and the conditions for the license to take effect.
It must also specify whether updates, support, maintenance, OEM redistribution,
sublicensing, or hosted-service rights are included.

**No annual subscription, perpetual license, unlimited company/group license,
reseller right, source-code ownership transfer, or future-version entitlement
is implied by the EUR 20,000 price.** Those details have not been preset.
No support level or warranty is promised by the public repository.

The price may be revised for future agreements. Existing executed agreements
and permissions already granted for noncommercial use are not retroactively
changed by editing this policy. Unsolicited payments and donations do not grant
commercial rights. Unauthorized use does not automatically create a EUR 20,000
debt or purchase a retroactive license.

## Contributors and third-party material

Commercial permission can cover only rights Christian Klose owns or is authorized
to sublicense. The [contributor agreement](CONTRIBUTOR-AGREEMENT.md) is designed
to obtain explicit, nonexclusive permission for accepted future contributions;
it does not automatically acquire rights to pre-existing third-party material.
Dependencies and separately licensed material retain their own terms.

Contributors retain ownership of their work. Participation does not itself promise
payment, equity, or a share of licensing revenue. Separate compensation can be
agreed explicitly; no such agreement is created by a pull request.

## Legal review

The community license and contributor agreement are project-specific texts, not
an unmodified standard license or a lawyer-reviewed contract. Obtain qualified
software-licensing advice before the first commercial agreement and substantial
external contributions, including review of rights ownership, enforceability,
research boundaries, contributor assent, and the eventual commercial contract.
